Model notes — the honest test table

Every value term, tested held-out twice

The value model is one term only: ridge (λ = 3) of log10(mcap/TVL) on log10(TVL), fitted on the 95 traded protocols with a known listing date and refit from the snapshot on every run. A term enters only when it lowers the held-out miss in both the leave-one-out and the walk-forward tests. Per-category mean ratios are never a feature — a category’s own mean leaks the answer for its members. Odds come from the peers’ cross-sectional record by age, not a forward hazard. Intervals use the leave-one-out residual spread (σ). Farm economics are net only: gross = implied × 5% pool × deposit share × odds; cost = gas + 2h × $15/h. “Miss” here is the geometric-mean miss: 10 to the mean absolute log10 residual, so ×4.36 means typically within a factor of 4.36 either way.
